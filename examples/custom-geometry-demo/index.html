Photonic custom geometry (F10 external geometry, F11 baked occlusion over it) Not baked.

The machined part in the middle was never built by a Photonic creator: it arrived as JSCAD-shaped polygon data (positions, flat normals, per-part colours) through scene.addGeometry. The red knob arrived as a caller-built group through scene.addMesh, and the green box as a raw indexed BufferGeometry with no normals. Tick the bake box and all of them take the same baked contact shading as the engine-made plinth. Drag to orbit.
