F1 complete: src/PhotonicOverlay.js adds PhotonicOverlay (plus createPoseOverlay, normalizePose, deviationColorHex, DEFAULT_OVERLAY_STYLE, POSE_CONNECTIONS, COCO17_JOINT_NAMES), exported from src/index.js. The overlay owns a separate transparent renderer (clear alpha 0, no scene background, no grid, no debug helpers) whose canvas exactly covers the video element's content rect (object-fit contain/cover/fill accounted for); poses are supplied in normalized video coordinates (name-keyed object, COCO 17 array, or joints/keypoints wrapper, with optional confidence gating) and mapped through an orthographic camera two world units tall, so world positions are independent of pixel size and pixel density. Default style is the KataPose MVP look: translucent cyan-blue cylinders with a fainter back-side shell as the outline for bones, and joint spheres with an additive halo as the glow; all style values are overridable via the constructor or setStyle. Positions are exact functions of the latest pose (smoothing off by default), so the overlay adds no jitter of its own. Deviation primitives: a target pose (setTargetPose or the setPose option) or per-joint offset/deviation data drives per-joint and per-bone color encoding by offset magnitude (cyan at/below the 0.05 warn threshold, blending to yellow at the 0.12 alert threshold, red beyond) and shaft-and-cone arrow glyphs whose direction and length equal the joint's 3D offset vector; arrows and color encoding toggle independently for the KataPose color-only-versus-arrows comparison. Design and limits are documented in design/OVERLAY.md and the README gained a summary section. Verification evidence below. Next: F2, virtual camera mode.
